    Benchmarking Runtime & Resource Tracking for Manuscript (Table 3 & Supplement)¶

    CRITICAL NOTE FOR MANUSCRIPT TABLES & BENCHMARKING SECTIONS When reporting total runtimes, speedup metrics, and resource footprints for CPU vs. GPU runs in Table 3 and the Supplementary Materials, the upstream alignment and deduplication runtimes from the original full runs MUST be included. The recent coverage-started runs were utilized to refresh downstream statistics, consensus DMRs, and Quarto rendering without needlessly wasting compute re-aligning terabytes of raw reads.

    • Segment 1: Initial alignment & processing run (desperate_bassi, Line 112): 2d 18h 46m 30s (~66.78 hours)
    • Segment 2 / Resumption: Completed tasks & downstream differential methylation (sleepy_salas / prickly_torricelli): 7h 28m to 7h 45m
